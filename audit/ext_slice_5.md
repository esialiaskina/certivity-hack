In the 02 series of amendments, the regulation is amended to account for vehicles equipped with an Automated Driving Systems (ADS [2] ). Recognizing the need for different arrangements for vehicles fitted with ADS, this regulation has been amended to clarify that DCAS is only permitted to operate when a driver is in control, and that vehicles without the ability to be controlled by a driver (vehicles of Categories X and Y) are not required to comply with this regulation. These changes were introduced based on the assumption that ADS vehicles must have superior capabilities in executing the lateral and/or longitudinal control of the vehicle on a sustained basis during normal driving and when avoiding collision and/or mitigating the crash severity in critical situations. In the case of vehicles equipped with an ADS other than vehicles of Categories X and Y, in the manual driving mode no special provisions or exemptions apply. 2 As defined in the Consolidated Resolution on the Construction of Vehicles (R.E.3.), document ECE/TRANS/WP.29/78/Rev.8, para. 2 https://unece.org/transport/standards/transport/vehicle-regulations-wp29/resolutions 1.
