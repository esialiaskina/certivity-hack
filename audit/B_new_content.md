[18] (sim_old=50) In the 02 series of amendments, the regulation is amended to account for vehicles equipped with an Automated Driving Systems (ADS [2] ).
[18] (sim_old=48) Recognizing the need for different arrangements for vehicles fitted with ADS, this regulation has been amended to clarify that DCAS is only permitted to operate when a driver is in control, and that vehicles without the ability to be controlled by a driver (vehicles of Categories X and Y) are not required to comply with this regulation.
[18] (sim_old=71) These changes were introduced based on the assumption that ADS vehicles must have superior capabilities in executing the lateral and/or longitudinal control of the vehicle on a sustained basis during normal driving and when avoiding collision and/or mitigating the crash severity in critical situations.
