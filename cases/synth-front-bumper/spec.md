# spec: Front Bumper System with Pedestrian Protection and Sensing

## Product Type

Front bumper system — exterior body component integrating pedestrian impact protection, radar sensor module, and front camera for advanced driver assistance.

## Vehicle Category

M1 — passenger car. System is designed to meet front underrun and pedestrian protection requirements for categories M1 and N1.

## Function

- Front impact protection (pedestrian and low-speed collision)
- Pedestrian lower-leg protection
- Energy absorption (bumper beam and foam)
- Radar sensing (adaptive cruise control and collision warning)
- Camera vision (lane-keeping assist and traffic-sign recognition)
- Aesthetic and aerodynamic body panel

## Main Parts

- Bumper fascia (outer cover)
- Energy absorber (foam block)
- Bumper beam (steel or aluminium cross-member)
- Radar module (77 GHz)
- Front camera module
- Mounting brackets
- Pedestrian protection foam
